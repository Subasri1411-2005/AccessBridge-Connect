    About AccessBridge AI

    Problem. People with speech, hearing or reading difficulties often cannot communicate quickly in everyday places such as hospitals, buses and shops, and most tools support English only.

    Solution. A free web app that speaks typed text, turns speech into large text, offers one-tap picture cards in Tamil and English, simplifies hard sentences and gives instant emergency help.

    Goal. UN Sustainable Development Goal 10: Reduced Inequalities.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    • Ctrl + Enter: speak the message
    • Esc: stop speaking or close the big screen
    • Alt + 1 to 6: switch sections

    Built with

    HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Web Speech API, localStorage, Service Worker (offline), ARIA accessibility, optional Claude API.

    Developed by M. Subasri, MCA, Rathinam Technical Campus. MCA Hackathon, domain: Accessibility and Inclusion.
